# Code of conduct

Everyone should be able to use, discuss, and contribute to Logseq Mobile free from harassment and discrimination. The [Logseq community code of conduct](https://github.com/logseq/logseq/blob/master/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) applies to this repository, including issue discussions, reviews, and interactions when representing the project.

## How we work together

- Treat people with kindness and respect, including when you disagree.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and make room for different experiences.
- Respect privacy and ask permission before sharing someone else's personal information.
- Keep harassment, threats, discrimination, and personal attacks out of project spaces.

## Report a concern

Contact a moderator privately through the [Logseq forum](https://discuss.logseq.com/) or another [official reporting channel](https://github.com/logseq/logseq/blob/master/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md#enforcement). Include what happened, where it happened, and any links or screenshots you feel comfortable sharing. The Logseq team keeps the reporter's identity confidential and may take action ranging from a private correction to removal from the community.
